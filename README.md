**The Coffee Shop**-

A text-based crafting adventure built with Python! 

Can you grow a tiny coffee stand into a $100 empire and catch the eye of the biggest coffee conglomerate in the world?

_🎮 The Story_-  
You start with nothing but a dream and some coffee beans. Serve a variety of quirky customers—from **Shrek** to **Sir Bartholomew the Third**—by mastering the art of the perfect brew.

_🚀 Features_
* **Dynamic Customer System:** Over 20 unique NPCs with random waiting times.
* **Crafting Ratios:** 9 different coffee types based on real-world ingredients (Base, Water, Milk, Sweetener).
* **Economic System:** Earn cash for perfect orders; lose money for "Yuck!"-worthy mistakes.
* **Cinematic Ending:** A full story finale with custom ASCII art.

_☕ The Menu_
Master these recipes to win:
* **Espresso:** Pure energy.
* **Latte:** Creamy and smooth.
* **Mocha:** For the chocolate lovers.
* **Cold Brew:** Refreshing and bold.
* **...and many more!**

_💻 How to Run_
1.  **Install Python:** Make sure you have Python installed on your computer.
2.  **Download:** Download the python file from this repository.
3.  **Open:** Open IDLE and open the file.


_🛠️ Built With_
* **Python 3**
* `time` and `random` libraries for realistic shop flow.
